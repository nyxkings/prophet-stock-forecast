IMPORTANT (academic honesty)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Your walk-forward comparative evaluation (2025-01-01 to 2026-06-01) does NOT
show that Prophet beat naive forecasting or that Prophet+MPT beat SPY.

Do NOT claim "my model is more accurate than all past works" using Figures C–E.
Those figures report real results and should be discussed honestly.

Use Figures A, B, F, G, H to argue that your *system design* is more complete
than typical prior single-method approaches.

SUGGESTED REPORT WORDING (SAFE)
================================================================================

"Unlike prior studies that typically examine forecasting or portfolio optimisation
in isolation, this project implements an integrated end-to-end system combining
Prophet forecasting, constrained mean–variance optimisation, risk analytics,
comparative evaluation, and a production dashboard (Figures A, B and F).

Empirical walk-forward results (Figures C–E) show that one-step Prophet forecasts
did not outperform naive or drift baselines on MAPE during 2025–2026, and that
the Prophet–MPT portfolio underperformed SPY over the same window. These findings
highlight both the value of rigorous baseline comparison and the practical
limits of short-horizon equity forecasting in a technology-concentrated universe."
